710 — Structural creak sound-effect credits

Eight edited game clips: creak_01.wav to creak_08.wav

Clips 01-04: Slowed hinge groans mixed with metal contraction.
Clips 05-06: Slowed hinge groans only.
Clips 07-08: Slowed metal contraction only.

These files are adapted mixtures of the sources. The hinge recording was slowed
and lowered, and metal-contraction noises were layered and processed. The
resulting mixtures were split into separate clips, level-adjusted, faded,
and resampled as mono 24 kHz IEEE float WAVs.
